Problem: "No signals detected"
  → Check DRY_RUN = True in config.py
  → Verify MT5 connection (check logs)
  → Check if symbol has tick volume
  → Wait 5+ minutes for first signal
  
Problem: "Too many false signals (W% < 50%)"
  → Increase CANDLE_IMBALANCE_VOLUME_RATIO to 0.65-0.70
  → Decrease ORDER_BLOCK_LOOKBACK to 15
  → Increase ORDER_BLOCK_PROXIMITY_TICKS to 5
  → Only trade signals with 70%+ confidence
  
Problem: "Missing good setups"
  → Decrease CANDLE_IMBALANCE_VOLUME_RATIO to 0.55
  → Increase ORDER_BLOCK_LOOKBACK to 30
  → Increase ORDER_BLOCK_PROXIMITY_TICKS to 15
  → Accept lower confidence signals (60-65%)
  
Problem: "Order blocks not triggering"
  → Check ORDER_BLOCK_PROXIMITY_TICKS = 10 (default)
  → Increase to 15 for wider entry zone
  → Verify consolidation detection in logs
  → Check that breakout happened cleanly
